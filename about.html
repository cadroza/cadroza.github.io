What is Cadroza?

Cadroza is a new fitness application designed to fit all the best features available into one place. With the plans to allow this app to be fully cross platform, with access over desktop, and both iOS and Android - it ensures that everybody has the opportunity to access the best.

What do we offer?

Calorie tracking.

We offer a calorie tracking system that allows for the use of AI food scanning. Users can take photographs of ingredients, or entire meals and the AI will then return the macros for the specific scan. (Please note that it is not 100% accurate. AI can and will make mistakes.) We also allow users to create a recipe book for meal prep, users can also access other users' recipe books (if that user has chosen for their recipe book to be shared publicly).

Workout and progress tracking.

We also offer a workout tracking service. Here users can log their workouts, no matter what - and see if they have made improvements across the weeks that they have been logging. Users can also log their body weight, and track their progress on whether they are losing or gaining weight. The user's maintenance calories will also vary based on the frequency and intensity that a user is logging exercises, if the user chooses to enable this option.
